How it works

Rules decide what's possible. AI only explains it.

Research for this case study found that first-time investors often lose their SIP without deciding to stop: a payment fails in a tight month, the alert doesn't say what to do, and a friend's advice decides what happens next. Groww's rules leave a narrow window: a skip within 2 days of the debit misses that month, cancelling is locked within 7 days, and 3 missed, failed or skipped instalments in a row cancel the SIP automatically.

Three layers

  1. Rules engine (code). Calculates every date, deadline and option from Groww's published help pages. The AI can't change these.
  2. AI explainer. Reads the question in the user's own words and explains their situation using only the engine's facts, citing rule IDs.
  3. Eval gate. Requests for fund picks or predictions are declined by code. Every AI answer then passes code checks (no fund names or advice, no predictions or urgency, only verified rules, dates and amounts that match) and an AI reviewer (facts, cancel described correctly, neutral). If anything fails, a safe fallback is shown instead.

What a non-AI version would miss

A fixed FAQ answers the question it expected. "Is my SIP gone?", "Will they charge me again?" and "agar cancel karun to paisa milega?" need different answers built from the same rules and the user's own dates.

Limits

  • Dummy data only. No account access; nothing here changes a real SIP.
  • Rules checked against Groww's help pages on 3–4 Nov 2026. Automatic retries, exact bank fees and fund-specific skip limits aren't documented, so the assistant says it can't confirm them.
  • Working days skip weekends but not bank holidays.
