Keep a SIP alive through a bad month

Many first-time investors lose their SIP without ever deciding to stop: a payment fails in a tight month, the alert doesn't say what to do next, and the SIP quietly lapses. This assistant steps in at those moments.

Pick a moment below, then tap the notification on the phone. Switch to Today to compare.

  1. 1A plain explanation first. Written by AI for this SIP, and checked before it's shown.
  2. 2Missed X of 3. Failed payments count toward the rule that cancels a SIP after 3 misses in a row. Now it's visible before it's too late.
  3. 3Options that fit the reason. Pick what's going on; each option shows its deadline for this SIP.
  4. 4Ask anything. In English or Hinglish. It never recommends funds or predicts returns; every answer is checked first.
  5. 5Cancel, explained. Still two taps, never hidden, and it first says the money stays invested.

      How it works

      Rules decide what's possible. AI explains it.

      First-time investors often lose a SIP without ever deciding to stop. A payment fails in a tight month, the alert doesn't say what to do next, and a few missed months later the SIP is gone. The Recovery Assistant steps in at those moments with the user's own dates and options.

      Try the live demoSee the quality report

      Three rules that decide whether a SIP survives

      3 in a rowThree missed, failed or skipped instalments in a row cancel the SIP automatically.
      2 daysA skip or edit within 2 days of the debit doesn't count for that month.
      7 daysCancelling is locked within 7 days of the next instalment.

      From Groww's published help pages, checked 3–4 Nov 2026.
