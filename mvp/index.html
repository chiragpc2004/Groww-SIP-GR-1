How it works

Rules decide what's possible. AI only explains it.

Research for this case study found that first-time investors often lose their SIP without deciding to stop: a payment fails in a tight month, the alert doesn't say what to do, and a friend's advice decides what happens next. Groww's published rules leave a narrow window: a skip within 2 days of the debit misses that month, cancelling is locked within 7 days, and 3 missed, failed or skipped instalments in a row cancel the SIP automatically.

Where it appears

  • Payment failed: from the failure notification, straight to the SIP with a Pay Now button and the "missed X of 3" tracker.
  • Tight month ahead: from the SIP screen, while a skip or edit still counts for this month.
  • About to cancel: the cancel flow first shows what happens to invested money, then cancels in two taps.

Three layers

  1. Rules engine (code). Calculates every date, deadline and option from Groww's help pages. The AI can't change these.
  2. AI explainer. Reads the question in the user's own words and explains using only the engine's facts, citing rule IDs.
  3. Eval gate. Fund-pick and prediction requests are declined by code. Every AI answer then passes code checks (no fund names or advice, no predictions or urgency, only verified rules, dates and amounts that match) and an AI reviewer (facts, cancel described correctly, neutral). If anything fails, a safe fallback is shown.

Limits

  • Dummy data only; nothing here changes a real SIP. Buttons that would act show a demo notice.
  • Rules checked against Groww's help pages on 3–4 Nov 2026. Automatic retries, exact bank fees and fund-specific skip limits aren't documented, so the assistant says it can't confirm them.
  • Working days skip weekends but not bank holidays.
